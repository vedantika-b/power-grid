How it works

Producer: power plants generate MW and put it into the grid.

Buffer: the grid holds at most capacity MW. Semaphore empty counts free MW, full counts MW waiting to be consumed, and a mutex guards both. A producer that finds empty = 0 blocks (overload).

Consumer: households, factories and offices take MW. If full = 0 they wait, so their demand is unserved (load shedding).

Throttling: when supply exceeds demand, producers cut output to what was consumed.

Supply = Σ producer output. Demand = Σ consumer demand. Difference = Supply − Demand. Utilization = grid load ÷ capacity × 100. Unserved = Demand − Supply when Supply < Demand; Excess = Supply − Demand when Supply > Demand.

JavaScript is single-threaded, so producers and consumers run as async tasks (Promise.all) that really contend for the mutex and semaphores. They are not OS threads.
